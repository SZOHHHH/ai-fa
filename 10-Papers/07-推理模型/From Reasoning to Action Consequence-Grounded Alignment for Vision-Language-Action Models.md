> 摘要（原文）：Reasoning informs decision-making by connecting intended actions with their expected consequences. Recent vision-language-action (VLA) models incorporate embodied chains of thought (ECoTs) to guide action generation. However, such guidance remains implicit, as actions are conditioned on ECoTs rather than explicitly optimized to align with them. We introduce CoRAct, a consequence-grounded reasoning-to-action alignment framework that explicitly aligns reasoning and action through reward-guided optimization in action noise space.
