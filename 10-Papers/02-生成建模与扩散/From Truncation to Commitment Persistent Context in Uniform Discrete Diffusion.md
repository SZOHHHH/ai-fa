> 摘要（原文）：Uniform-state discrete diffusion models update all tokens in parallel while keeping every position revisable. Even when the commonly used top-$p$ rule leaves only one candidate at a position, that choice affects only the current reverse step and can be revised at the next sampling step. We ask what changes when selected hypotheses instead become persistent context for later predictions. We therefore propose committed reveal sampling (CRS), a training-free sampler that stores selected argmax tokens and inserts them into subsequent model inputs.
