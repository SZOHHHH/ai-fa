> 摘要（原文）：Emergency vehicle detection in autonomous driving is a safety-critical perception task that demands robustness under diverse and adverse real-world conditions. Existing approaches rely on a single modality, either audio or video, which leads to systematic failure when that modality is degraded: microphone-based systems fail in noisy urban environments, and camera-based systems fail at night or under occlusion.
