> 摘要（原文）：Monitoring a model's chain of thought (CoT) is useful for safety only if the final answer depends on the written steps. Prior measurements use natural tasks where true reasoning is unknown. We measure it against an exact ground truth on a synthetic box-swap task where every intermediate state is known.
