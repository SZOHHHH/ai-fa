In this work, we introduce LaPla, a unified Vision-Language-Action (VLA) framework featuring latent-aligned planning to seamlessly ground semantic understanding in precise motion execution. We first design an action tokenizer based on a residual vector-quantized variational autoencoder (VQ-VAE), capturing vehicle kinematics and encoding trajectory features into a structured latent space.
