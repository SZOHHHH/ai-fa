> 摘要（原文）：Unified multimodal models can both generate and understand images within a single set of weights, offering an opportunity to use image synthesis as training supervision for visual comprehension. However, existing visual chain-of-thought methods rely on human-annotated helper images during training and require slow image generation at test time. We present SelfThinker, the first self-evolving framework that turns a unified model's self-generated visual thoughts into stronger understanding without human image annotations.
